CBSE System

Curriculum & Methodology

Maharishi Vidya Mandir, Red Hills, provides a robust academic framework aligned with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), New Delhi. Our teaching methodology focuses on active learning, enquiry-based exploration, and standard intellectual development.

We believe in keeping class strengths small, enabling teachers to understand the intellectual speed and uniqueness of each child. Regular formative assessments are structured to evaluate understanding rather than rote memorization capacity.

Along with national CBSE textbooks, we provide supplementary reading materials and practical kits to help our young scholars relate theoretical knowledge to daily-life applications.

Beyond Academics

Co-Curricular & Extracurricular Programs

We nurture students' unique artistic and physical abilities alongside standard class teaching.

Fine Arts & Crafts

Weekly classes in sketch-work, canvas painting, and paper crafts. Helps stimulate visual imagination and fine motor coordination.

Yoga & TM

Daily practice sessions dedicated to breathing (Pranayama), yoga postures, and Transcendental Meditation for brain development.

Physical Training

Structured sports coaching focusing on athletics, gymnastics, and popular outdoor games to cultivate muscle agility and group teamwork.

Public Speaking

Regular classroom presentations, reciting competitions, and dramatic story-telling events to develop stage presence and language command.

Grades Breakdown

Grade-Wise Syllabus Overview

Take a quick look at the subjects and learning objectives map for each section.

  • Kindergarten (LKG & UKG)

    +

    Our Kindergarten syllabus is built around visual-tactile play modules that teach letters, numbers, and basic motor skills:

    Focus Areas Key Learning Objectives
    Languages Phonics, basic letter tracing, sight words, storytelling, and nursery rhymes in English.
    Mathematics Counting up to 100, pattern recognition, basic shapes, and size-comparison guides.
    General Awareness Understanding self, family, parts of body, animals, weather, and basic safety rules.

    Integrated daily activity: 10 minutes of light breathing (Pranayama) and storytelling for stress-free brain relaxation.

  • Primary Foundation (Class I to Class III)

    +

    Primary grades introduce standard desk-learning and analytical subject divisions mapped under CBSE guidelines:

    Subjects Curriculum Outline
    English & Regional Language Grammar basics, paragraph writing, reading comprehension. Introduction of Tamil/Hindi as second languages.
    Mathematics Arithmetic basics (addition, subtraction, multiplication tables), fractions intro, measurement units, and simple word problems.
    Environmental Studies (EVS) Living & non-living things, plant anatomy, water cycles, air pollution basics, and historical stories.

    Integrated daily activity: 15 minutes of Transcendental Meditation (TM) and stretching exercises (Asanas) before morning classes.

  • Upper Primary (Class IV to Class VI)

    +

    Prepares students for analytical reasoning, deeper science modules, and structural language rules:

    Subjects Curriculum Outline
    English & Tamil/Hindi Advanced essay formats, literature prose analysis, spelling bees, and creative story-writing tasks.
    Mathematics Decimals, percentages, intro to algebra, basic geometry (angles, areas), and handling data graphs.
    Science & Social Science Physics basics (motion, light), Chemistry basics (matter states), Biology basics (food chains). Geography maps and history timeline systems.
    Computer Science Computer system anatomy, MS office tools, internet safety basics, and scratch programming concepts.

    Integrated daily activity: 20 minutes of daily TM and Yoga practice to release school stress, ensuring absolute focus and mental growth.
