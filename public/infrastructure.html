A Contemporary Learning Environment

Maharishi Vidya Mandir, Red Hills, features an inspired campus architecture thoughtfully designed to nurture intellectual curiosity, physical vitality, and peace of mind through Consciousness-Based Education.

Aerial Campus Masterplan

Sustainable Masterplan & Eco-Conscious Campus

CLICK PHOTO TO ENLARGE

Our expansive campus features a thoughtfully organized layout that seamlessly integrates indoor academic spaces with outdoor natural learning landscapes. The masterplan highlights rooftop solar power arrays for clean energy generation, wide permeable grass-paver pathways, extensive open-air assembly grounds, and dedicated play zones surrounded by native green tree buffers.

Rooftop Solar Arrays Clean, renewable energy powering classrooms
Permeable Grass Pavers Eco-friendly rainwater-harvesting walkways
Spacious Play Grounds Dedicated sports courts and sand play arena
Entrance Colonnade

Welcoming Entrance Pavilion

An inviting entrance portico crafted with natural brick cladding, smooth white plaster pillars, circular architectural viewports, and shaded covered walkways. Designed to provide a secure and inspiring daily arrival experience for students and parents.

  • • Granite Planter Borders

Academic Wing

Modern Academic Wing

The academic building incorporates contemporary passive climate design. Vertical powder-coated mustard yellow louvers deflect direct solar glare, while artistic circular and square jali screens promote continuous gentle cross-ventilation.

  • • Mustard Climate Louvers
  • • Perforated Jali Screens
  • • Naturally Lit Corridors
  • • Glazed Toughened Windows
Front Elevation

Architectural Elevation & Rhythm

A harmonious exterior facade balancing warm earth-toned brick tile finishes with crisp modern geometry. Large classroom windows invite ample natural daylight, creating an uplifting, stress-free atmosphere essential for focused learning.

  • • Natural Brick Finish
  • • Energy Efficient Glazing
  • • Grey Textured Concrete
