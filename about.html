About Our School

Home > About Us

VISION

To provide consciousness-Based Education unfolding every student’s full potential - intellectually, emotionally, physically, spiritually. To integrate Transcendental meditation, yoga and Pranayama into regular schooling for stress-free learning environment.

MISSION

The school aims at holistic education encompassing the development of all the domains of the students and to improve the performance of the students every year in such a way that all the students get into high profile reputed institutions. It ensures to improve the students’ overall health through physical and mental fitness programs.

VEDIC FOUNDATION

The Essence of Consciousness-Based Education

Maharishi Vidya Mandir, Red Hills, believes that education must not only inform but also transform. Our unique methodology incorporates the Science of Creative Intelligence and Maharishi Vedic Science into the standard CBSE framework.

By cultivating the inner source of intelligence and creativity, our students naturally experience less stress, think more clearly, make better choices, and absorb knowledge much faster than in traditional schooling systems.

Our daily routines include short sessions of Transcendental Meditation (TM), breathing exercises (Pranayama), and simple Yoga postures. This practice relaxes the nervous system, preparing the child to receive and process academic lessons in a highly receptive mental state.

Daily Practice

Integrated Wellness Elements

How we construct a stress-free environment for students every day.

Transcendental Meditation

A simple, natural, effortless technique practiced twice a day. TM allows the mind to settle down to a state of quiet alert restfulness, reducing fatigue, increasing clarity, and improving academic performance.

Yoga & Pranayama

Structured breathing exercises and physical postures help students release stored nervous tension. These simple daily practices enhance body alignment, improve flexibility, and increase oxygen flow to the brain.

Balanced Development

Our mental and physical fitness routines monitor and nurture the student's health. We combine academic tasks with active play, ensuring a balanced growth index and improving the overall immune response of our pupils.

Goals

Our Core Objectives

Key academic, personal, and developmental objectives we target for every student.

A

Academic Brilliance

Achieve complete mastery of subjects mapped under the CBSE curriculum with interactive learning tools, regular assessments, and conceptual clarity in mathematics, sciences, and languages.

B

Inner Strength & Calm

Cultivate students who stay calm under pressure, resolve peer issues harmoniously, and maintain focus in their work by utilizing the daily stress-free routines.

C

Physical Wellness

Enable healthy lifestyle habits through regular sports, yoga routines, and physical activities designed to build motor skills, muscle strength, and agility in our young learners.

D

Social Responsibility

Nurture values of kindness, mutual respect, cleanliness, and environmental care. We prepare students to think globally and act locally for a sustainable and happy future.
