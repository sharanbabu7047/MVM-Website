Enrollment Guidelines

Admission Policy & Process

Admissions at Maharishi Vidya Mandir, Red Hills, are open to all qualifying students subject to seat availability. We nurture a diverse and inclusive learning community following the standard CBSE parameters.

Eligible Grades

For the current academic year, applications are accepted for the following grades:

  • Kindergarten (LKG & UKG)
  • Primary Classes (Class I to Class V)
  • Middle School Foundation (Class VI)

Enquiry Procedure

  1. Fill out the Admission Enquiry Form on the right.
  2. Our administrative department will review your enquiry details and get in touch with you via email or phone.
  3. An interactive session/school tour will be scheduled for parents and the student.
  4. Formal document verification and final fee payments complete the enrollment process.
