Campus Amenities

Nurturing Growth with Rich Amenities

We provide standard resources to help children explore subjects practically and play actively.

Computer Laboratory

Our computer lab is equipped with high-speed computers running child-safe systems. We teach students the basics of operating systems, office applications, and computational logic through interactive visual programming frameworks like Scratch.

  • • 1:1 Student to PC ratio
  • • Child-safe web filtering
  • • Modern LED monitors
  • • Hands-on coding modules

Library & Reading Room

A peaceful storage of wisdom containing age-appropriate children's fiction, science guides, maps, history encyclopedias, and CBSE reference tools. The library encourages a lifetime reading habit among pupils from Kindergarten itself.

  • • Curated story collections
  • • Monthly educational magazines
  • • Quiet study cubicles
  • • Weekly library slots

Sports Facilities & Yoga Hall

Our school features an open outdoor playground for physical drills and team games. Additionally, we have a quiet, carpeted indoor Yoga Hall where daily morning Transcendental Meditation and Yoga posture practice are conducted.

  • • Indoor TM/Yoga arena
  • • Athletic track lanes
  • • Outdoor football/cricket nets
  • • Professional physical coaches

Transportation Network

We operate a fleet of modern school buses covering major residential clusters near Red Hills. Every bus is staffed with a trained driver, a helper, and follows strict security check-points to ensure child transit safety.

  • • Verified routing points
  • • Speed governor limits
  • • Attendant present in every bus
  • • Periodical safety drills
